{: .note-title }
> 2A > 5C Frame Trap 
>
> For opponents that press buttons during defense
<details><summary></summary>
After your opponent starts to throw tech from your 2A Strike/Throw, the gap between 2A and 5C will catch someone pressing. After 5C you can go for 5B to help hit-confirm and keep you even on block. After 5B is a good spot to reset back to 2A Strike/Throw, 2A>5C frame trap, or another block string.

{: .note-title }
> 5C whiff 5A
>
> Resetting pressure from a distance
<details><summary></summary>
You need enough buttons to push you out of range of 5A before this works. Buttons like 2B and 2C slightly advance Linne forwards. Whiffing 5A after 5C(or2C) will leave you even on block. This will let you use farther pressure tools like Dash attacks, air fireball or far normals like 5C and 2C.

> Using Specials on block instead of to combo
